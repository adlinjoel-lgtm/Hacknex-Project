Behavioural Assessment

The account assessment compares current activity against historical behaviour. A high transaction value alone does not automatically indicate fraud. Additional indicators, account history and relationship evidence should be considered before escalation.

Customer Assistance

Guidance for customers affected by a suspicious transaction or account security concern.

Transaction Concern

If a transaction was not authorised by the account holder, the customer should immediately contact the financial institution through its verified official support channel.


Account Security

Customers should review recent transactions, protect account credentials and report activity they do not recognise.


Recommended Operational Actions
1 Verify the transaction with the account holder.
2 Review recent account activity.
3 Check beneficiary and device relationships.
4 Document the investigation decision.
5 Escalate only when sufficient evidence exists.
